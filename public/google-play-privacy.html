Fideora · Google Play Privacy

INFO CURSOS NEGOCIOS DIGITAIS LTDA · Updated 6 November 2026

1. Scope and contact

This policy covers the prepared Android version of Fideora. The controller can be contacted at support@fideora.app. The app is intended for adults; it does not target children.

2. Local content and optional accounts

Fideora stores preferences, reading and prayer progress, notes and intentions on the device. With an optional email account, profile and progress can be synchronized to Supabase. Notebook and intention sync is off initially and is enabled only by your explicit choice. Notes and prayer activity can reveal religious beliefs; do not include unnecessary information about other people. Optional email sign-in uses Supabase for authentication. Email, account UUID, authentication/session information and necessary security logs are processed to operate your account. Passwords, email codes and auth credentials are never included in analytics or support logs by the app.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Google Play processes Android payment transactions. RevenueCat validates purchases, restores access and manages the Premium entitlement. It processes an anonymous customer identifier or the signed-in account UUID, purchase/subscription records, product, price/currency, status and expiry, and necessary SDK request metadata. We do not receive card details and do not send journal/intentions text, names or email attributes to RevenueCat. Store and service-provider records are subject to their own retention obligations.

4. Analytics, permissions and service providers

Native RevenueCat paywall interactions and subscription lifecycle information support the purchase experience and offer measurement. Personal notes and intentions are not sent to that paywall service. No advertising SDK, advertising ID collection, Health Connect, location, camera or microphone feature is enabled by this preparation. Google, RevenueCat, Supabase and transactional email/hosting infrastructure process relevant data as service providers. Integrations must be rechecked before release. Data sent to these services uses HTTPS/TLS; cloud processing may occur outside your country. Account auth secrets use native secure storage; this is not a claim of end-to-end encryption for all local notes.

5. Legal bases and choices

We process account and subscription information to provide the requested service, operational/security records for service reliability and security, optional synchronization with your choice, and records required by applicable legal obligations. Depending on your location you may request access, correction, export, deletion or other applicable privacy rights. Contact support using only the information necessary for your request.

6. Retention and deletion

Use Profile to export local data and delete the account. Account deletion removes the associated profile, progress and synchronized notebook/intention records. Local data on other devices must also be removed there. Disabling sync stops new uploads; it does not delete existing cloud copies. Service-provider backups may take their normal rotation period to expire. Limited transaction/security records may be retained for accounting, legal, fraud-prevention or security needs. We do not promise instantaneous erasure of all provider backups. Request deletion without reinstalling the app using the account deletion page. Deleting an app account does not cancel the store subscription or issue a refund.
